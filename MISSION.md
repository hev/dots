# Mission

**Voice dots that people actually work through: one per person, over their
own sources, that say nothing they can't source and act only after a yes.**

This is the mission of the `dots` line (the DOT team in Linear) and of its
repos: hev/dots, the open framework, and the dots built on it: hev/dot (hev
mind's), hev/me (Adam's), hev/dasha (Dasha's) and hev/bcc-dot (Story's).
Two clauses, and each is a test a plan has to pass.

## A tool for working

A dot earns its place by being used: Adam runs his day through his, Dasha
through hers, and a client asks theirs instead of asking Adam. The measures
are how often a dot is opened, how many questions it answers without a
fallback to a laptop, and how many acts it carries out after a spoken yes.
A plan says which dot it makes more useful to which person, or it goes back.

A new dot is a copy of the last one's shape: its own repo, its own theme, an
inbox at `<name>@in.hevdots.com`, a site on hevdots.com. What two dots both
need moves into hev/dots; what only one needs stays in its repo.

## A complement to Layer

Layer is the default source and the reason a dot can answer questions over a
whole corpus. A dot that needs better retrieval files that gap on LYR and
works through Layer, never around it. A dot must still work with no Layer
namespaces at all, because most of what a person works through (calendar,
mail they forward, money, photos) is not a corpus.

## What this rules in

- Anything that makes a dot someone already uses more useful to them.
- Lifting what a second dot copied from the first into the framework.
- A new dot for a named person who will use it.

## What this rules out

- Framework features no running dot uses.
- Retrieval built inside a dot or the framework instead of filed on Layer.
- Hev mind or client specifics in hev/dots.

## How the machine reads this

The coordinating model reads this file when it plans work on this line, and
judges what it is about to start, and what is already in flight, against a
named clause. RFCs on the DOT team are drafted against it.
